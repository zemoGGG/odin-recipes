  • Onions
  • Carrots
  • Yellow Potatoes
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • Chuck roast, chicken thighs, pork cutlet
  • Black Pepper
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Chicken/Beef Broth
  • Soy Sauce
  • Worcestershire Sauce
  • Curry Roux Package
  • Short grain rice to serve
  1. Mince onions, or cut into wedges.
  2. Peel carrots, and cut at a 45 degree angle, rotating a quarter turn every cut.
  3. Peel potatoes, cut into quarters. Soak in water.
  4. Grate the ginger, mince the garlic.
  5. Cut chuck roast into 1 inch pieces, chicken thighs into bite size pieces, or prepare pork cutlet for frying.
  6. Into large pot, heat oil over medium heat.
  7. Saute onions for 5 minutes, add garlic and ginger.
  8. Add broth, soy sauce, worcestershire sauce, other condiments.
  9. Add carrots and potatoes, cover and simmer for 15 minutes.
  10. Skim the foam off the top while it is boiling, and cook until potatoes are tender.
  11. Remove from heat. Add curry roux one block at a time until it dissolves.
  12. Simmer until roux becomes thick, and season to taste. Cook pork cutlet at this point if necessary.
  13. Serve with a side of white rice.
