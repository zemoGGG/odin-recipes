General Tso's Chicken

    Marinade
  • Egg White (1)
  • Dark Soy Sauce (2 tbsp/30 ml)
  • Shaoxing Wine/Dry Sherry (2 tbsp/30 ml)
  • Vodka (2 tbsp/30 ml)
  • Cornstarch (3 tbsp/24 g)
  • Baking Soda (1/4 tsp/1 g)
  • Chicken Breasts/Thighs (1 pound, cut into 1/2-3/4 inch pieces)

    • Dry Coating
  • All-Purpose Flour (1/2 cup/64 g)
  • Cornstarch (1/2 cup/64 g)
  • Baking Powder (1/2 tsp/2 g)
  • Kosher Salt (1/2 tsp/1.5 g)

  • Oil for frying (1.5 qt)

    • Sauce
  • Sugar (4 tbsp/50 g)
  • Chicken Stock (3 tbsp/45 ml)
  • Dark Soy Sauce (3 tbsp/45 ml)
  • Shaoxing Wine/Dry Sherry (2 tbsp/30 ml)
  • Chinese rice vinegar/Distilled white vinegar (2 tbsp/30 ml)
  • Cornstarch (1 tbsp/8 g)
  • Peanut/Vegetable/Canola Oil (2 tsp/10 ml)
  • Garlic (2 cloves, minced)
  • Ginger (1 inch piece, minced)
  • Scallions (1-2, white part only, cut into 1 inch pieces)
  • Sesame Oil (1 tsp/5 ml)
  • Dried Arbol/Chinese Chiles (8, small)

  • Fresh Steamed Rice
    Marinade
  1. Beat egg whites in a large bowl until lightly foamy. Add soy sauce, wine, and vodka. Whisk to combine.
  2. Pour half of the mixture into a small bowl to use for the dry coat later.
  3. Add cornstarch and baking soda to the large bowl, and whisk to combine.
  4. Add chicken pieces, and use your hands to coat thoroughly. Cover with plastic and set aside.

    5. Dry Coat
  5. Combine flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl. Whisk to combine.
  6. Add the reserved marinadem ixture, and whisk until clumps are formed. These will make your chicken extra crispy when frying.

    7. Sauce
  7. Combine sugar, chicken stock, soy sauce, wine, vinegar, cornstarch, and sesame oil in a small bowl. Stir with a fork until everything is dissolved and no lumps remain.

    8. Frying Chicken
  8. Heat oil in a large wok or Dutch oven to 350°F.
  9. Working one piece at a time, transfer chicken to the dry coat, tossing in between to completely coat chicken.
  10. Once all pieces are added, toss and press the mixture into the chicken with your hands so it adheres. Make sure each piece is coated thoroughly.
  11. Once piece at a time, shake off excess coating and add to the hot oil. Once all the oil is added, agitate with a metal spider so the pieces don't stick together.
  12. Maintaining an oil temperature around 350°F, cook until chicken is cooked through and very crispy.
  13. Transfer to a paper towel-lined bowl to drain.

    14. Finishing
  14. Combine oil, garlic, ginger, and red chiles in a wok or large skillet and place over medium heat. Cook, stirring frequently until vegetables are aromatic but not browned.
  15. Stir the sauce mixture and add to the wok. Stir until the sauce boils and thickens, around 1 minute. Add scallion segments to the wok.
  16. Add chicken and toss until all pieces are thoroughly coated.
  17. Serve immediately with white rice.
