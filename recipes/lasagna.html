Lasagna

  • Italian Saugage (1 lb, sweet)
  • Ground Beef (1 lb)
  • Onion (1/2 cup, minced)
  • Garlic (2 cloves, minced)
  • Crushed Tomatoes (28 oz)
  • Tomato Sauce (13 oz)
  • Tomato Paste (12 oz)
  • Water (1/2 cup)
  • Sugar (2 tbsp)
  • Parsley (4 tbsp, fresh, chopped)
  • Basil (1 1/2 tsp, dried)
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Italian Seasoning (1 tsp, to taste)
  • Black Pepper (to taste)
  • Lasagna Noodles (12)
  • Ricotta Cheese (16 oz, whole milk)
  • Mozzarella Cheese (3/4 lb, shredded)
  • Parmesan Cheese (3/4 cup, grated)
  • Egg (1)
  1. Cook sausage, ground beef, and garlic in a large pot over medium high heat until cooked through.
  2. Add crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, tomato paste, water. Add sugar, parsley, basil, salt, Italian seasoning, pepper. Simmer for 1 1/2 hours, covered, stirring occasionally.
  3. Bring a large pot to boil. Cook lasagna noodles for 1-2 min less than the package says. Drain and rinse with cold water.
  4. In a medium bowl, combine ricotta, egg, parsley, salt.
  5. Preheat oven to 375 F.
  6. Add a layer of sauce to the bottom of a 9x13 pan. Add noodles lengthwise. Add ricotta, mozarella, meat sauce, then parmesan.
  7. Repeat until out of noodles, and top with rest of mozarella and parmesan.
  8. Spray a piece of foil and cover the pan.
  9. Bake for 25 minutes, remove foil and bake for 25 more minutes. Let rest for 15 minutes before serving.
