Neapolitan Pizza Dough

    Recipe makes 4x 12" pizzas, or 3x 16" pizzas
  • Bread Flour (600g)
  • Kosher Salt (12g)
  • Yeast (9g, instant)
  • Water (390g, 85°F-90°F)
  1. Mix all ingredients together in a large bowl until no dry ingredients remain.
  2. Cover with plastic and let rest at room temperature for 8-12 hours.
  3. Divide into 4 dough balls for 12" pizzas, or 3 dough balls for 16" pizzas.
  4. Place each dough into lightly oiled individual containers, cover and refridgerate for 2-4 days.
  5. 3 hours before cooking, shape dough into balls and let rest at room temperature. After this, they are ready to cook.
