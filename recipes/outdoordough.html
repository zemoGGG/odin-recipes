Outdoor Oven Pizza Dough

    Recipe makes 4x 12" pizzas, or 3x 16" pizzas
  • All-Purpose Flour (535g)
  • Kosher Salt (12g)
  • Yeast (8g, instant)
  • Water (360g, 40°F)
  1. Pulse flour and yeast together in food procesor. With machine running, add water and mix until rough ball forms. Cover with towel and let rest for 20 mins.
  2. Add salt to top of dough, process for 20-30 seconds longer. Continue pulsing until the internal temperature of the dough is 75°F.
  3. Transfer to lightly oiled bowl, form loose ball, and cover for 45 mins.
  4. Knead dough in bowl for 10-20 seconds until smooth. Cover at room temp for 1-2 hours until nearly doubled.
  5. Divide into 4x 225g dough balls for 12" pizzas or 3x 300g dough balls for 16" pizzas.
  6. Put each into lightly oiled containers. Cover and refridgerate for 1-4 days.
