★ Pepper Steak

Difficulty
★ ★ ☆ ☆ ☆
  • London Broil, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • Onion (1, minced)
  • Garlic (3 cloves, minced)
  • Bell Peppers (1/4 cup, frozen)
  • Beef Stock (2-3 cups)
  • Worcestershire Sauce

    • Corn Starch Slurry
  • Corn Starch (1 tbsp)
  • Water (cold)
  1. Heat 1 tbsp of oil over medium high. Add the meat, season with salt and pepper, and saute until it browns. Remove the meat.
  2. Over medium heat, add 1 tbsp oil. Saute onions until transluscent, 3-4 minutes. Add garlic and saute for 30 seconds.
  3. If there is still fond left on the bottom of the pan, deglaze with a little bit of broth and scrape up with a wooden spoon.
  4. Add meat back to pan, add bell peppers, and add the broth just until it covers the meat. Add 1 tbsp of worcestershire sauce.
  5. Bring to a boil, then simmer over low heat until meat is tender (45-60 minutes)
  6. When meat is tender, bring to a boil over medium-high and add cornstarch slurry while constantly mixing. Continue adding until thickened.
  7. Serve over white rice or egg noodles.
