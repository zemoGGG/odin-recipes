title: Japanese Curry
author: matty
category: mains
status: published
specialty: true
image: /images/recipes/curry.png
image_alt: Japanese curry with rice
ingredient_groups:
  - items:
      - Onions
      - Carrots
      - Yellow potatoes
      - Ginger
      - Garlic
      - Chuck roast, chicken thighs, or pork cutlet
      - Black pepper
      - Vegetable oil
      - Chicken/beef broth
      - Soy sauce
      - Worcestershire sauce
      - Curry roux package
      - Short grain rice, to serve
step_groups:
  - steps:
      - Mince onions, or cut into wedges.
      - Peel carrots, and cut at a 45 degree angle, rotating a quarter turn every cut.
      - Peel potatoes and cut into quarters. Soak in water.
      - Grate the ginger and mince the garlic.
      - Cut chuck roast into 1 inch pieces, chicken thighs into bite-size pieces, or prepare pork cutlet for frying.
      - In a large pot, heat oil over medium heat.
      - Sauté onions for 5 minutes, then add garlic and ginger.
      - Add broth, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, and other condiments.
      - Add carrots and potatoes, cover and simmer for 15 minutes.
      - Skim the foam off the top while it is boiling, and cook until potatoes are tender.
      - Remove from heat. Add curry roux one block at a time until it dissolves.
      - Simmer until roux becomes thick, and season to taste. Cook pork cutlet at this point if necessary.
      - Serve with a side of white rice.
