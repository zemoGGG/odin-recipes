ingredient_groups:
  - items:
      - Cooked white rice
      - Vegetable oil
      - Onion
      - Garlic
      - Soy sauce
      - Sesame oil
      - Kosher salt and black pepper
      - Egg
      - Fresh vegetables (carrots, etc.)
      - Frozen vegetables (peas, etc.)
step_groups:
  - steps:
      - For best results, use day-old rice OR make fresh rice and spread it flat on a tray to rest before cooking.
      - Mince onion, garlic, and other fresh vegetables.
      - Heat oil in a wok over high heat until smoking. Add rice and toast for 3 minutes, working in portions.
      - Return rice to wok, create a well in the middle and add oil. Add onion, garlic, and fresh vegetables and sauté for 1 minute.
      - Add soy sauce along the sides of the wok and toss. Add sesame oil and toss. Season with salt and pepper.
      - Move rice to one side and add oil. Add egg and scramble, then mix together.
      - Add frozen vegetables and serve immediately.
