title: Lasagna
author: matty
category: pasta

specialty: false
image: /images/recipes/lasagna.png
image_alt: A slice of layered meat lasagna
ingredient_groups:
  - name: Meat sauce
    items:
      - Italian sausage (1 lb, sweet)
      - Ground beef (1 lb)
      - Onion (1/2 cup, minced)
      - Garlic (2 cloves, minced)
      - Crushed tomatoes (28 oz)
      - Tomato sauce (13 oz)
      - Tomato paste (12 oz)
      - Water (1/2 cup)
      - Sugar (2 tbsp)
  - name: Seasoning
    items:
      - Parsley (4 tbsp, fresh, chopped)
      - Basil (1 1/2 tsp, dried)
      - Salt (to taste)
      - Italian seasoning (1 tsp, to taste)
      - Black pepper (to taste)
  - name: Assembly
    items:
      - Lasagna noodles (12)
      - Ricotta cheese (16 oz, whole milk)
      - Mozzarella cheese (3/4 lb, shredded)
      - Parmesan cheese (3/4 cup, grated)
      - Egg (1)
step_groups:
  - steps:
      - Cook sausage, ground beef, onions, and garlic in a large pot over medium-high heat until cooked through.
      - Add crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, tomato paste, and water. Add sugar, parsley, basil, salt, Italian seasoning, and pepper. Simmer for 1 1/2 hours, covered, stirring occasionally.
      - Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook lasagna noodles for 1–2 minutes less than the package says. Drain and rinse with cold water.
      - In a medium bowl, combine ricotta, egg, parsley, and salt.
      - Preheat oven to 375°F.
      - Add a layer of sauce to the bottom of a 9x13 pan. Add noodles lengthwise. Add ricotta, mozzarella, meat sauce, then parmesan.
      - Repeat until out of noodles, and top with the rest of the mozzarella and parmesan.
      - Spray a piece of foil and cover the pan.
      - Bake for 25 minutes, remove foil and bake for 25 more minutes. Let rest for 15 minutes before serving.
