author: matty

image: /images/recipes/neapolitandough.jpg
image_alt: Two Neapolitan-style pizzas with charred, puffy crusts
servings: 4× 12 inch or 3× 16 inch pizzas
ingredient_groups:
  - items:
      - Bread flour (600g)
      - Kosher salt (12g)
      - Instant yeast (9g)
      - Water (390g, 85–90°F)
step_groups:
  - steps:
      - Mix all ingredients together in a large bowl until no dry ingredients remain.
      - Cover with plastic and let rest at room temperature for 8–12 hours.
      - Divide into 4 dough balls for 12 inch pizzas, or 3 dough balls for 16 inch pizzas.
      - Place each dough into lightly oiled individual containers, cover and refrigerate for 2–4 days.
      - 3 hours before cooking, shape dough into balls and let rest at room temperature. After this, they are ready to cook.
