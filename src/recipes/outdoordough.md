title: Outdoor Oven Pizza Dough
author: matty

image: /images/recipes/outdoordough.jpg
image_alt: Pizza going into an outdoor pizza oven
servings: 4× 12 inch or 3× 16 inch pizzas
ingredient_groups:
  - items:
      - All-purpose flour (535g)
      - Kosher salt (12g)
      - Instant yeast (8g)
      - Water (360g, 40°F)
step_groups:
  - steps:
      - Pulse flour and yeast together in a food processor. With the machine running, add water and mix until a rough ball forms. Cover with a towel and let rest for 20 minutes.
      - Add salt to the top of the dough, and process for 20–30 seconds longer. Continue pulsing until the internal temperature of the dough is 75°F.
      - Transfer to a lightly oiled bowl, form a loose ball, and cover for 45 minutes.
      - Knead dough in the bowl for 10–20 seconds until smooth. Cover at room temperature for 1–2 hours until nearly doubled.
      - Divide into 4× 225g dough balls for 12 inch pizzas or 3× 300g dough balls for 16 inch pizzas.
      - Put each into lightly oiled containers. Cover and refrigerate for 1–4 days.
